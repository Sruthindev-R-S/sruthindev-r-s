## About

I'm a Computer Science student at the **School of Engineering, Cochin University of Science and Technology (CUSAT)**, interested in building software and understanding the systems behind it.

My interests span **backend engineering, full-stack development, artificial intelligence, machine learning, Linux, and systems programming**. I prefer learning by building projects, experimenting with technologies, and understanding what happens beneath the abstractions I use.

Currently, I'm focused on strengthening my fundamentals while working on practical projects involving APIs, databases, distributed systems, AI/ML, and software infrastructure.

That approach has led me to explore everything from data structures and algorithms to databases, networking, containers, AI models, and operating systems.
